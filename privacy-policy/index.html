    Privacy Policy

    What this website does, and does not do, with your information.

    Last updated: 6 November 2026

    Who we are

    This website is run by Panambara Business Solutions, 240/A, Old Railway Street, Eheliyagoda, Sri Lanka. You can reach us at info@panambara.lk or on 071 666 4111.

    What this website collects

    This website has no user accounts and does not set advertising or tracking cookies of its own. Our contact form does not store anything: it opens WhatsApp or your email app with your message ready to send. What you send is then handled by that service and by us.

    Content from other services

    To work, this site loads some things from other providers, which may receive technical details such as your IP address and browser type under their own privacy policies:

    • Google Fonts for the typefaces on every page.
    • Google Maps on the contact page.
    • YouTube: video thumbnails load on the home page, and the video player loads only after you tap a reel.

    How we use what you send us

    When you contact us we use your details to reply, to discuss and provide the services you ask for, and to keep the records the work requires.

    Sharing

    We do not sell your information. We share it only where needed to carry out work you ask us to do, for example when we make a filing with a government authority on your behalf, or where the law requires it.

    How long we keep it

    We keep client records for as long as we need them to do the work and to meet our legal and professional obligations.

    Your choices

    You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to what we are required by law to keep. Write to info@panambara.lk.

    Changes

    We may update this policy; the date above shows when it last changed.
