    Common questions

    Can you register a new company for me?

    Yes. Company registration is part of our company secretarial service.

    What are statutory records?

    The official records a company is legally required to keep up to date. We maintain them for you.

    Do you help after the company is registered?

    Yes. That includes annual returns, board and shareholder documentation and ongoing compliance support.
